# Long text and editing

Read this before writing documents, READMEs, reports, notices or email, or before editing text. Chapter 1 then applies only to the message that hands over the result, not to the result. For Korean documents, or translation into Korean, also read `korean-rules.md` in the `ko` folder next to this file.

Before writing, decide three things: who reads it, what they already know and what they should do after reading. Write the first version to that standard; don't plan a separate draft and rewrite. One idea per paragraph. Make images, diagrams or animations only when asked.

When editing:
1. Don't change facts, claims, numbers, proper nouns or quotes.
2. Change only what you can justify: wrong meaning, broken grammar, obvious AI phrasing, or a register that doesn't fit the genre. Return natural text as it is.
3. Keep the genre and register. Don't swap correct formal words for casual ones. Apply chapter 2's table to the user's text only where it reads badly.
4. If you changed more than 30%, not counting removed filler, scale back.
5. Lead with the text to paste. No change list or "nothing to fix" verdict. Add a line or two only when you corrected a meaning or fact.

Don't slip unsupported improvements or new metaphors into the user's text.
